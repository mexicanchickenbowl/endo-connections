# 31 — External inflammatory (infection-related) root resorption · hazard placard

Resorption series 3/5. Silent, 21.9 s (unchanged after review), 1080x1920, one 2D canvas (`index.html` + `reel.js`), deterministic `render(t)`.
Evidence brief: `RESEARCH.md` (researched + independently fact-checked). Only its section (b) "recommended on-screen
facts" is used on screen, with its wording constraints and its "Do not claim" list.

## Concept
The whole reel is one industrial safety placard. The disease is written as a signage equation
(two hazard diamonds add up to one warning triangle), the mechanism is a cutaway panel with a countdown-style dial,
the treatment is a blue mandatory-action header plus a red lock-out tag, and the takeaway is a mandatory sign:
**treat the canal — early**. Take either term out of the equation and the result is struck out.

## What is schematic (labelled on screen: `SCHEMATIC · NOT TO SCALE`, `TIMELINE SCHEMATIC`)
- Toxin particles, their number and speed; the red tint on "open" tubules. Diffusion through tubules is a
  histology-based theory (Andreasen 1981), not a measurement.
- Layer proportions (cementum and PDL are enlarged), cell size and number, crater depth and shape, growth rate.
- The dial: no scale, no rate; only the three text anchors carry information. Spacing is not proportional to time.
  The dial carries the qualifier `AFTER TRAUMA` (fine print): true for all three rows (replanted monkey incisors;
  400 replanted human incisors; a review of traumatic injuries). The specifics are in the row text and the caption.
- Speed and extent of repair. The crater is lined by cementum-like tissue and the PDL space/bone follow the new
  contour; the lost dentin is not refilled (RESEARCH.md correction 10).
- The lamp matrix is a literal 192 lit / 5 hollow of 197; the "97%" is never shown as a partial count.
- Equation recap on the end card (chips `DAMAGE` + `INFECTION`, blue cancel bars) is a visual restatement of claims 1
  and 8, not an additional claim.
- Hook: the lacunae popping in over 1.2 s are a title animation, not a time course.

## Design rationale
- Style: safety yellow / black with red as the hazard accent and ISO-style blue reserved for the mandatory action
  (treatment). Anton for sign lettering, Barlow Condensed for plates and fine print. GHS-type diamonds for the
  two causes, an ISO-type warning triangle for the result, an ANSI-type header for the action, a lock-out tag
  for "stimulus cut", and a blue mandatory circle for the takeaway. Chevron tape frames the sign and doubles as the
  scene wipe.
- Layout: all must-read text sits in a 64–950 px column so nothing enters the Instagram button column; the right
  gutter is filled with marching chevrons instead.
- Colour is semantic everywhere: red = infection/clastic activity, black = hard-tissue outlines and cementum,
  cream = dentin, blue = disinfected/treated.
- One source line per scene: the timer scene lists its three time anchors in row order plus the tubule theory
  (`Timeline: Andreasen 1980 · Andreasen et al. 1995 · Patel 2022 (review) · Tubule theory: Andreasen 1981`, 24 px);
  the same slot then carries Cvek 1992. Journals are in the caption Refs line (all four, since the citation audit;
  before it `Patel 2022` and `Andreasen 1981` had no journal anywhere the viewer could see) and in the table above.
