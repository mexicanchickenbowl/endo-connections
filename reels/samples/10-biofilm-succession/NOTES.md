# 10 · Root Canal Takeover (biofilm succession), 8-bit pixel-art time-lapse

**Output:** `reels/out/10-biofilm-succession.mp4` (26.0 s, 1080×1920, 30 fps, silent) + poster `reels/out/10-biofilm-succession.jpg` (t = 19.6 s)
**Style slot:** pixel art / 8-bit retro game. Everything is drawn into a fixed **180×320 indexed pixel buffer**, then scaled **×6 with no smoothing**. The 48-colour palette is fixed: no alpha blending and no gradients, and every pixel is one palette colour. Tones and gradients come from **4×4 Bayer ordered dithering** (pulp, bone, O₂ gradient, enamel shading, biofilm). The only non-pixel element is a static CRT overlay drawn on top: pixel-grid scanlines and a vignette.

## Concept
The canal is played as a game in "levels". Each level is a later time point of the classic sealed-canal monkey experiment (Fabricius et al. 1982). Little pixel microbes swim in from the mouth, divide (binary-fission slide-outs from a neighbour) and take over. A retro HUD shows the level, a rolling **DAY** counter (time-lapse ►►) and an **O₂ meter** that drains to empty. Under the tooth, a stacked "share of cells" bar morphs from mostly **facultative (yellow)** to mostly **obligate anaerobes (magenta)**, and from Level 4 it shows the measured **85–98 %** as a hatched range, not a point. The ending is a **LEVEL COMPLETE** card with the takeaway. The reel needs no sound.

Colour code (kept consistent everywhere): **colour = oxygen tolerance, shape varies** (yellow/orange = facultative cocci and rods · magenta/purple = obligate-anaerobe rods, spirochetes and cocci) · cyan = O₂ · green = peptides · pink = periapical exudate · brown = necrotic pulp debris.

## What is schematic / exaggerated (also stated on screen)
- On-screen disclaimers: "SHARE OF CELLS · ILLUSTRATIVE" and "NOT TO SCALE · TIME COMPRESSED" (panel, Levels 1–3), "APICAL, DAY 90-1060 · SCHEMATIC BAR" (panel, Level 4 and end card), "OPEN ~1 WK (MODEL)", "SEALED · STUDY MODEL", "ILLUSTRATIVE 9:1", "3 MONKEYS · 24 CANALS · CULTURE".
- Tooth anatomy is a generic single-rooted tooth. The pulp chamber and canal are **greatly widened** so the microbes are visible, and bacteria are thousands of times larger than true scale.
- Sprite counts, positions and growth curves are a toy model. Only the final apical range (85–98 %) is data, and it is drawn as a range.
- The O₂ meter, O₂ particles, teal→purple lumen gradient, tissue-breakdown timing, peptide pellets, exudate droplets, biofilm thickness and lesion size are all illustrative.
- Time is compressed from about 3 years into about 20 s, and the DAY counter rolls between the study's sampling points.

## Design rationale
- **Pixel-art fidelity:** one grid (6 px cells) for everything, including type. Large text is Press Start 2P rasterised at 8/16 px and **alpha-thresholded** to hard pixels; its stylised "8" read as an "S", so a plain 8 bitmap is stamped into its monospace cell. Fine print uses an **original 3×5 bitmap micro-font** (`microfont.js`), whose "8" now has closed bowls (`###/#.#/###/#.#/###`) so "1982" and "85-98%" never read as "19S2"/"S5-9S%". Cap height 30 px at 1080 width meets the ≥30 px rule.
- **Game grammar = narrative grammar:** levels are time points, the HUD shows the changing variables (day, O₂), the dialog box carries the caption, banners make the time jumps, and LEVEL COMPLETE delivers the takeaway. The handle is the dialog's speaker tag and is repeated on the end card.
- **Retro motion:** sprite jitter and float run at a stepped 6 fps (×3 during fast-forward), not on every 30 fps frame, so the canal no longer "boils". Only the lone facultative icon bobs on the end card.
- **One focal point at a time:** title → HUD → banner → dialog are staggered; Level 3 is only the O₂ crash + anaerobe bloom (one tag, after the banner); Level 4 carries biofilm, lesion and exudate with one tag at a time.
- **Safe areas:** all readable text sits in x 64–1016, y 230–1580. Nothing readable is at x > 960 below y 1100 (panel ends at x 959; end-card lines below grid y 184 stay at x ≤ 159; the BIOFILM tag ends at x 153). The title box top border is at y 252, inside the 3:4 crop.
- **Determinism:** `render(t)` is pure. Slots, noise and particles come from `Kit.rng`, `Kit.hash` and `Kit.noise`. Births and deaths are precomputed from the count curves. The whole frame is one canvas (fast capture path).

## Known limitations
- Per-time-point percentages (6 mo vs 3 yr) were not verifiable from the abstract, so the reel deliberately shows a range only. The 85–98 % figure itself should be confirmed against the Wiley full text before posting.
- "Exudate" as a nutrient source rests on a PubMed-indexed abstract (Sundqvist 1992, nutritional supply) plus a review that was not checked in full text.
- The DAY 7 share is illustrative (not in any abstract checked).
- 26 s is at the upper end of the 22–26 s target; the extra time goes to the title (+0.4 s) and the end card (5.6 s).
