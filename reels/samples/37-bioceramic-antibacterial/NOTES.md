# 37 · Bioceramics: bactericidal or bacteriostatic — and for how long?

Style: **AGAR PLATE LAB BENCH** — top-down petri dishes on a matte black bench; amber and blood agar, stippled lawns,
glass rims with soft-box reflections, grease pencil (Patrick Hand) and lab label tape (IBM Plex Mono).
Duration 24.0 s, 1080 x 1920, silent. Poster = t 22.4 s (complete summary frame).
Evidence: `RESEARCH.md` (fact-checked brief). Nothing is on screen that is not in its recommended / verified lists.

## Concept
Four dishes sit in a 2 x 2 grid on the bench. A camera visits them one at a time — each dish answers one part of
the question — then pulls back so all four are visible under a three-line verdict. The answer is deliberately
model-specific: every number on screen sits next to its material and its test.

## What is schematic (and labelled so on screen)
- Dish 1: the pink halo (drawn like a pH indicator), the hydroxyl-ion dots and the size of the clear zone —
  yellow tape "halo + zone: schematic". The pH numbers are measurements of MTA cement with a pH meter, not of this
  plate; the 2-min kill is a direct contact test, not an agar diffusion zone. The pH tape and the kill note are kept
  apart and no arrow links them (Zhang 2009: pH alone did not explain the effect).
- Dish 2: halo size and the dashed "fresh" reference ring. The absence of a zone is the measured finding.
- Dish 3: tubule geometry, number of cocci and the fractions turning red (about 14% -> 30% -> 53%) are
  illustrative — yellow tape "live/dead stain · counts schematic". Only the direction (more dead at day 30) and
  "partial" are sourced. Death order is shuffled so that no depth gradient is implied.
- Dish 4: colonies and both curves — fine print "curves + colonies schematic".
- Bars in dish 2 are true to scale (0-100%); the percentages appear only when each bar has finished growing.

## Design rationale
- One physical world. Dishes, timers, tapes and pencil notes are objects on the same bench; the camera pans
  between them and pulls back, so the end card is literally the bench with everything on it.
- Palette: matte black bench, amber agar with cream lawn, phenolphthalein pink for alkalinity, blood-agar red for
  the definitions plate, green/red fluorescence in the loupe; wax white and wax yellow for handwriting; off-white
  and yellow tape. Yellow tape is reserved for qualifiers ("schematic", "mostly in vitro", "SET ?").
- Time is carried by a bench timer beside dishes 1-3 so no extra time captions are needed.
- Neighbouring dishes fill the top and bottom no-read zones as decoration; all must-read text stays inside
  x 64-1016, y 230-1580 and clear of the button column.
