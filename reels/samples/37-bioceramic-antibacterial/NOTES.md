## Concept
Four dishes sit in a 2 x 2 grid on the bench. A camera visits them one at a time — each dish answers one part of
the question — then pulls back so all four are visible under a three-line verdict. The answer is deliberately
model-specific: every number on screen sits next to its material and its test.

## Caveats kept off screen (caption / here)
- "Fresh" on the end card = iRoot SP vs planktonic E. faecalis only (Zhang 2009). MTA showed no effect on strict
  anaerobes by agar diffusion (Torabinejad 1995, J Endod 21:403-6, PMID 7595152, abstract).
- Fresh versus set is NOT a measured contrast for MTA: crushed set MTA powder was "either equally or more
  effective than fresh powders" against E. faecalis (Zhang H, Pappen FG, Haapasalo M. J Endod 2009;35(2):221-4,
  PMID 19166777, abstract). Dish 2 therefore says only what Farrugia 2017 measured: partial kill by set MTA that
  declines from day 1 to day 7 of aging.
- "KILLED" on the dish 2 tape is shorthand: the paper measured a reduction in viable counts against a
  polystyrene control and notes that some bacteria may adhere to the material.
- Only the water arm is on screen. Aged in blood: 82.64% (0.82 log) at day 1 and no different from control at
  day 7 (5.41 vs 5.59 log10, p > 0.05) — "this antimicrobial activity was lost" (Farrugia 2017, full text). This
  is in the caption.
- The bars are true to scale in percent, which looks stronger than it is microbiologically (1.67 log and under
  1 log); "<2 log" is in the caption.
- Cidal/static is a laboratory label. The antibiotic-testing convention is a 99.9% (3 log10) fall at 18-24 h; it
  is not a cement standard, agents classed bacteriostatic can still lower counts, and the label depends on
  conditions. The convention figure is NOT taken from the Pankey & Sabath abstract (paper body not read); it is
  cited here only as a laboratory convention and is not on screen.
- An agar zone means no growth, not killing; it is read at 24-48 h (the bench timer's 0 h to 3 h belongs to the
  pH tape only) and it cannot separate bactericidal from bacteriostatic (Farrugia 2017 Sci Rep, full text).
- In tubules the effect is not bioceramic-specific: BC Sealer and AH Plus did not differ at any time point (Wang
  2014 abstract). No percentages for BC Sealer are verified.

## What is schematic (and labelled so on screen)
- Dish 1: the pink halo (drawn like a pH indicator), the hydroxyl-ion dots and the agar zone — yellow tape "agar
  zone = no growth · schematic". The lawn starts faint (freshly seeded) and thickens everywhere except in the
  zone; no grown lawn is erased. The pH numbers are measurements of MTA cement with a pH meter, not of this plate;
  the 2-min kill is a direct contact test, not an agar diffusion zone. The pH tape and the kill note are separate
  objects, shown one after the other, and no arrow links them (Zhang 2009: pH alone did not explain the effect).
- Dish 2: the small residual halo and its dots — fine print "halo schematic". The absence of a zone is the
  measured finding. Bars are true to scale (0-100%); each percentage appears only when its bar has finished.
- Dish 3: tubule geometry, number of cocci and the fractions turning red (about 14% -> 53%) are illustrative —
  yellow tape "live/dead stain · counts schematic". Only the direction (more dead at day 30) and "partial" are
  sourced. Death order is shuffled so that no depth gradient is implied.
- Dish 4: colonies and both curves — fine print "curves + colonies schematic". The falling curve stops above the
  axis ("falls", not "to zero").

## Design rationale
- One physical world. Dishes, timers, tapes, handle scraps and pencil notes are objects on the same bench; the
  camera pans between them and pulls back, so the end card is literally the bench with everything on it. Fine
  print is small grease pencil (32 px), not floating type.
- Palette: matte black bench, amber agar with cream lawn, phenolphthalein pink for alkalinity, blood-agar red for
  the definitions plate, green/red fluorescence in the loupe; wax white and wax yellow for handwriting; off-white
  and yellow tape. Yellow tape is reserved for qualifiers ("schematic", "IN VITRO", "CLINICAL", "SET: LESS").
- Time is carried by a bench timer beside dishes 1-3 so no extra time captions are needed.
- One focal point at a time: in every scene the text is finished before the timer sweep / data beat starts.
- Neighboring dishes fill the top and bottom no-read zones as decoration; all must-read text stays inside
  x 64-1016, y 230-1580 and clear of the button column (end-card right rims end at x 953).
