# 35 — The separated file (forensic case file / evidence board)

## Concept
A detective's cork board builds the "case" of a separated NiTi file. The world is the finished board at exactly
1080 x 1920, so the last frame is the complete, legible summary; the camera zooms into clusters and follows the red
thread: case -> two mechanisms -> suspects -> prevention -> prognosis -> decision.
Duration 24.0 s, silent. About 61 must-read words. Poster = t 1.5 s (title + circled fragment, reads at grid size).

## On-screen claims and sources (all verified on PubMed; abstract unless stated; details in RESEARCH.md)
1. Two mechanisms, torsion and flexural fatigue — Sattapan et al., J Endod 2000;26:161-5 (PMID 11199711). Wording
   "tip binds, shaft turns" and unwinding as plastic deformation: Ohri, Chien & Peters, Materials 2024;17:6147
   (PMID 39769746, PMC full text, narrative review; "clinical conditions are likely a combination of the two").
2. "may unwind" / "often no warning" — hedged on purpose: most clinically fractured files showed no visible
   deformation whatever the mechanism (Spanaki-Voreadi et al., Int Endod J 2006, PMID 16507069; Peng et al., Int
   Endod J 2005, PMID 16011774). Fracture at mid-curve: Pruett et al., J Endod 1997;23:77-85 (PMID 9220735, in vitro).
3. tight curve, big file — Pruett 1997 (in vitro; smaller radius, larger angle and larger instrument = fewer cycles).
   big file = fatigue in a curve only (Pruett 1997); small files fail more by torsion: in Shen 2009 (below) 34 of 48
   defects were in size 20. The source line is therefore typed on both cards and the thread reaches them from FATIGUE only.
4. no glide path — Berutti et al., J Endod 2004;30:228-30 (PMID 15085052; resin blocks, ProTaper S1: mean 10 blocks
   without vs 59 with manual preflaring; numbers not on screen; the same paper found instruments "worked better at
   high torque", so it is cited for the glide path only). forced — authors' inference in Sattapan 2000 plus expert
   consensus (Fan et al., Int J Oral Sci 2025, PMID 40484859); shown as a suspect, not a cited finding.
5. reuse — Gomes et al., Aust Endod J 2021;47:372-85 (PMID 33410578; use in more than four teeth associated with
   more fractures); debated, no validated safe number of uses.
6. operator (ringed, no rank) — Parashos, Gordon & Messer, J Endod 2004;30:722-5 (PMID 15448468): 7,159 discarded
   instruments from 14 endodontists; the operator was the most important influence on DEFECT rates (unwinding 12%
   plus fracture 5%), stronger than instrument design. It was not compared with curvature, glide path, force or
   reuse, and the outcome is not fracture incidence in patients, so no "#1".
7. Prevention ticks — glide path: Berutti 2004 (in vitro; the on-screen line sits under this row only).
   light touch: expert opinion (see 4). discard early: Shen, Coil & Haapasalo, J Endod 2009;35:193-6
   (PMID 19166771; small files single-use); Gomes et al., Aust Endod J 2021;47:372-85 (PMID 33410578; use in more
   than four teeth associated with more fractures). No validated safe number of uses. (Parashos 2004 does not
   support discarding by use count: instruments with defects had fewer mean uses, 3.3 vs 4.5.)
8. Healed 91.8% (fragment) vs 94.5% (control), n.s., follow-up at least 1 year — Spili, Parashos & Messer,
   J Endod 2005;31:845-50 (PMID 16306815); 146 matched pairs, specialist practice; healing as assessed by the
   authors on masked radiographs. Bars are drawn to the true values on a 0–100% scale. Not an equivalence result:
   with a pre-operative lesion healing was 86.7% vs 92.9% (n.s., subgroup size not in the abstract).
9. Removed or bypassed, by curve: before 100%, beyond 31% — Shen, Peng & Cheung, Oral Surg Oral Med Oral Pathol
   Oral Radiol Endod 2004;98:605-10 (PMID 15529134); 72 teeth in total ("72 teeth in all" on screen), a variety of
   techniques; at the curve 60% (caption); subgroup sizes not in the abstract. Context, not on screen: referral
   series with microscope plus ultrasonics report 87% overall (Suter et al., Int Endod J 2005;38:112-23,
   PMID 15667633, 84 of 97) and 95% (Cujé et al., Int Endod J 2010;43:545-54, PMID 20456518, 162 of 170), so 31%
   is a 2004 mixed-technique figure, not a current benchmark.
10. "Infection drives the outcome." — expert statement: Lin, Rosenberg & Lin, JADA 2005 (PMID 15782522); a fragment
    matters because it may hinder disinfection. The three tabs are equal options: no guideline exists and the
    decision is case-based (stage of preparation, periapical status, fragment position) — Madarati, Hunter &
    Dummer, J Endod 2013;39:569-81 (PMID 23611371). Leaving the fragment is an option, not a rule.

Thread links (torsion -> no glide path/forced; fatigue -> tight curve/big file; all -> operator) are a schematic grouping.

## Schematic / decorative
Radiograph, both mechanism animations (rotation slowed, proportions exaggerated), pictograms and the L-scale are
schematic; "schematic" is typed on the cards. Flutes are drawn as a right-handed helix (rising to the right), which
matches the clockwise arrow, the flutes travelling coronally and the unwinding when the tip binds. Fatigue exhibit:
red = tension side, blue = compression side; the crack is drawn fixed on the tension side for legibility; in
reality it rotates with the instrument, opening and closing each turn.
Decoration, not content: CASE NOTES stamp, EVIDENCE bag (carries the handle), markers 1–6, spare white string.
Left off screen on purpose: mechanism percentages, incidence (Iqbal 2006), lesion subgroup numbers, Souter & Messer.

## Design
Cork, manila, legal pad, index cards; Special Elite typed text, Permanent Marker notes, Anton stamps/tabs; one red
thread, red/green/navy inks, warm lamp vignette. Files: index.html, tex.js, art.js, reel.js.

  the source for the whole checklist.

  fixes OOOOE reading as 0000E in the typewriter face). Caption gives all three levels (all / 60% / 31%) and
  "mixed techniques, 2004". Suter 87% and Cujé 95% added above as context.
- The marker arrow from "n.s." to LEAVE + MONITOR is deleted: the three tabs stand as equal options.
- "#1" removed from the operator card (ring kept); claim and caption reworded to "defects (unwinding or fracture)".
- "big file" carries "Pruett 1997, in vitro" like "tight curve".

  to Refs, at-the-curve 60% added.
- Discard-early sources aligned between NOTES and meta.json (Shen 2009, Gomes 2021; not Parashos 2004).
- Flute helix mirrored to right-handed (also on the evidence-bag pieces); crack/colour convention noted above.
