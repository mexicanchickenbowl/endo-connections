# 35 — The separated file (forensic case file / evidence board)

## Concept
A detective's cork board builds the "case" of a separated NiTi file. The world is the finished board at exactly
1080 x 1920, so the last frame (and poster, t = 22.2 s) is the complete, legible summary; the camera zooms into
clusters and follows the red thread: case -> two mechanisms -> suspects -> prevention -> prognosis -> decision.
Duration 23.0 s, silent. About 61 must-read words.

## On-screen claims and sources (all verified in RESEARCH.md; abstract unless stated)
1. Two mechanisms, torsion and flexural fatigue — Sattapan et al., J Endod 2000;26:161-5 (PMID 11199711). Wording
   "tip binds, shaft turns" and unwinding as plastic deformation: Ohri, Chien & Peters, Materials 2024;17:6147
   (PMID 39769746, PMC full text, narrative review).
2. "may unwind" / "often no warning" — hedged on purpose: most clinically fractured files showed no visible
   deformation whatever the mechanism (Spanaki-Voreadi et al., Int Endod J 2006, PMID 16507069; Peng et al., Int
   Endod J 2005, PMID 16011774). Fracture at mid-curve: Pruett et al., J Endod 1997;23:77-85 (PMID 9220735, in vitro).
3. tight curve, big file — Pruett 1997 (in vitro; smaller radius, larger angle and larger instrument = fewer cycles).
4. no glide path — Berutti et al., J Endod 2004;30:228-30 (PMID 15085052; mean 59 vs 10 resin blocks, ProTaper S1,
   numbers not on screen). forced — authors' inference in Sattapan 2000 plus expert consensus (Fan et al., Int J
   Oral Sci 2025, PMID 40484859); shown as a suspect, not a cited finding.
5. reuse — Gomes et al., Aust Endod J 2021 (PMID 33410578); debated, no safe number of uses.
6. operator "#1" — Parashos, Gordon & Messer, J Endod 2004;30:722-5 (PMID 15448468): most important influence on
   DEFECT rates (unwinding or fracture) in 7,159 discarded instruments.
7. Prevention ticks — glide path first (Berutti 2004); light touch (expert opinion, see 4); discard early
   (Parashos 2004; Shen et al., J Endod 2009, PMID 19166771).
8. Healed 91.8% (fragment) vs 94.5% (matched control), n.s., follow-up at least 1 year — Spili, Parashos & Messer,
   J Endod 2005;31:845-50 (PMID 16306815); 146 matched pairs, specialist practice; healing as assessed by the
   authors on masked radiographs. Bars are drawn to the true values on a 0–100% scale.
9. Removed or bypassed: before the curve 100%, beyond 31% — Shen, Peng & Cheung, OOOOE 2004;98:605-10
   (PMID 15529134); 72 teeth, mixed techniques; subgroup sizes not in the abstract (at the curve: 60%).
10. "Infection drives the outcome." — expert statement: Lin, Rosenberg & Lin, JADA 2005 (PMID 15782522); the
    fragment matters because it blocks disinfection. Decision is case-based: Madarati et al., J Endod 2013 (PMID 23611371).

Thread links (torsion -> no glide path/forced; fatigue -> tight curve/big file; all -> operator) are a schematic grouping.

## Schematic / decorative
Radiograph, both mechanism animations (rotation slowed, proportions exaggerated), pictograms and the L-scale are
schematic; "schematic" is typed on the cards. Decoration, not content: CASE NOTES stamp, EVIDENCE bag, markers 1–6.
Left off screen on purpose: mechanism percentages, incidence (Iqbal 2006), lesion subgroup numbers, Souter & Messer.

## Design
Cork, manila, legal pad, index cards; Special Elite typed text, Permanent Marker notes, Anton stamps/tabs; one red
thread, red/green/navy inks, warm lamp vignette. Files: index.html, tex.js, art.js, reel.js.
