# 06 · Why cover the cusps? — BLUEPRINT

Silent 25.6 s reel (1080×1920, 30 fps). Output: `reels/out/06-cuspal-coverage.mp4` and poster `reels/out/06-cuspal-coverage.jpg` (poster at t = 11.95 s: stage C under full load, bar C full).
The length is slightly above the brief's 20–24 s target. That is the cost of meeting `Kit.readSecs` for every caption, including the longer, conditional takeaway that the review asked for (see Review changes).

## Concept
The whole reel is one engineering drawing sheet that animates, like a load-test report. A premolar is shown in
bucco-lingual **section 1–1**, mounted crown-up in a test mount, with strain gauges just above the CEJ (the method
Reeh et al. used). A loading sphere and rod apply the occlusal load. The sphere wedges between the cusp inclines, and the cusps splay outward.
Next to the section are an **occlusal view** and a live **cusp-stiffness bar chart**. In the occlusal view the marginal ridges are drawn as
engineering **tie members** between the cusps. The drawing goes through four revisions (REV A–D): intact → endo access + RCT → MOD → MOD with a
cuspal-coverage onlay. In each revision the load is applied again and δ is drawn against a dashed undeformed ghost outline. Only after the load
peaks does a signal wire run from the strain gauge to the chart and that revision's bar fill. The reel then shows the clinical outcome (6×, with a
ratio pictogram) and ends on a conditional takeaway.

The key point is made visually. In the section, access and MOD look almost the same. During the MOD cut the occlusal view zooms up as a
drafting "detail" (section dimmed), both ridge ties **snap**, and amber revision clouds mark them. Then the cusps splay (stiffness 37 % of intact).

## What is schematic or exaggerated (all disclosed on screen)
- **δ is exaggerated** and not to scale. On-screen: "δ ∝ 1/stiffness / exaggerated" (top-left), title block "SCALE: NTS" and "NOTE: δ & STRAIN ZONES SCHEMATIC", and General Note 1. No numeric scale factor is shown.
- The drawn δ follows δ ∝ 1 / relative stiffness (17 px intact, 17.9 px access, 46 px MOD, 8 px onlay) at the same load. The deformation field (cusps hinging about the cervical third) is a toy model. Reeh measured cervical strain, not cusp-tip displacement. Because the splay is exaggerated, the sphere sinks onto the MOD cavity rims at stage C, a contact that would not occur at real scale (deflections are around 10 µm).
- Coral strain zones are schematic dashed contours (no FEA-like glow), and the gauge colour change is schematic.
- Anatomy is simplified (a maxillary second-premolar-like B–L section, single canal, generic access, MOD and onlay). Coral hidden lines in stage C are the proximal boxes behind the section plane.
- The ties in the occlusal view are a mechanical metaphor for the marginal ridges, not a measured structure.

## Design rationale
- **Style: BLUEPRINT.** Deep cyanotype blue with subtle mottling, fine 16/80 px grid and vignette, sheet border with zone letters/numbers and centring marks. White and pale-cyan line art. **Share Tech Mono** for headers, numbers and labels, **IBM Plex Mono** for captions and fine print.
- **Engineering-drawing grammar, used not imitated:** section + occlusal view tied by section line 1–1 (view arrows toward M, so buccal is on the left as drawn; B/L letters in both views), material-specific hatching, centreline ℄, hidden lines, a cylinder break on the load rod, an axis break on the 211 % bar, δ dimension lines against a ghost outline, leader and elbow callouts, a **DETAIL · SCALE 3:2** view, **revision triangles** (deltas) whose letter is struck through and superseded, **scalloped revision clouds** with matching triangle tags, a title block, general notes, and an **APPROVED** stamp.
- **Colour carries meaning:** white = geometry, cyan = reference/annotation, amber = load, markup and focus hand-off, coral = loss and strain, mint = restoration and coverage.
- **One focal point per beat:** cut (zoomed detail for MOD) → load and splay → wire → bar. Captions withhold the number until the load test starts.
- **Motion:** eased throughout. A plotter crosshair draws the tooth and the pictogram teeth. Cuts sweep top-down, GP fills apex-up, the onlay drops in, and ties snap with an outBack spring. Gentle 0.5 Hz load cycle at the end. Nothing flashes.
- **Layout:** all readable content is inside x 64–1010 and y 250–1580. The chart (x up to 1010) ends at y 1096, above the Reels button column (y > 1100). General notes below the title block are decorative.
- **Text sizes:** title 86 px, captions 41 px, caption tags 32 px, chart title 30 px, chart values 29 px, chart subtitle 24 px, citations 22–23 px, callouts 22 px.

## Files
- `index.html` (page contract) and `reel.js`: one full-frame canvas with a deterministic `render(t)`. The background, a feathered dim mask and the stamp texture are pre-rendered in `setup()`.
- Geometry is in millimetres (x buccal−/lingual+, y up from the CEJ), at 44 px/mm. The sphere's rest position is solved every frame against the deformed crown and cavity walls, and blended across surface changes while it is lifted, so it never jumps.

## Review changes (v4, after independent science + design review)
**Science**
- **Takeaway made conditional (must).** It now reads "POSTERIOR ENDO TOOTH, RIDGE LOST? / COVER THE CUSPS. / onlay or crown", following the ESE 2021 position statement (coverage when a proximal wall is lost; not needed for a Class I cavity with adequate walls). It no longer contradicts REV B. ESE 2021 and Mannocci 2002 were added to the claims table.
- REV B copy: "Access alone:" → tag "ENDO ACCESS + RCT", line 1 "Ridges intact:", line 2 "only −5% stiffness". The −5 % now carries its condition, and the tag and body agree.
- Bar D: added a dashed "other study" divider before D, changed the stage tag to "CUSPAL-COVERAGE ONLAY (CAST GOLD*)", and changed the footnote to "*D: separate in-vitro series · cast-gold onlay = 2.11× intact".
- 6×: the source block now reads "adjusted HR 6.0 (95% CI 3.2–11.3) · 203 teeth / retrospective · all tooth types · association only", and the pictogram header adds "TOOTH LOSS, ANY CAUSE". All of this is inside the safe area (the old General Note 3 was not).
- Final source line: "Reeh 1989 (J Endod; J Dent Res) · Aquilino 2002 / Linn & Messer 1994 · ESE position statement 2021".
- F readout: the 111 N value is shown only for the J Endod series. From REV D on it reads "F" (the J Dent Res load could not be verified).
- δ note: "δ ∝ 1/stiffness / exaggerated". The title block now says "δ & STRAIN ZONES SCHEMATIC".
- Section-line view arrows flipped to point toward M, which is consistent with buccal on the left. "B" and "L" were added under the section.

**Design**
- Chart: every bar now shows % ("100%", "95%", "37%", "211%*"). The chart was widened to x 700–1010 with bars 42 px, and the subtitle is 24 px at full opacity. Labels near 100 % are lifted 12 px above the reference line. Each letter has an occlusal pictogram (intact / access / MOD slot / mint onlay) so the final frame explains itself.
- MOD focal point: the occlusal view zooms ×1.5 into a DETAIL panel during the cut (section and chart dimmed 42 %), with amber revision clouds and a "C" tag. Hidden box lines are coral at 0.9 with a "PROX. BOXES (BEHIND)" callout.
- Marginal ridges are drawn as tie members with tension arrows (A, B). They snap at the MOD cut, with coral broken ends springing apart. In D they become a mint tie plate with restraining arrows.
- Captions in two beats: the number types when the load starts (B 6.35, C 10.37, D 14.62), not before.
- Tags shortened (no "REV X —" prefix) and set at 32 px, full opacity. Caption A starts at 1.7 s. A and B were lengthened, and the hover and approach/retract times were cut.
- No blink at stage changes: the revision triangle stays, the old letter is struck through in amber and replaced, the old text slides up while fading, and the new text types right after.
- D re-timed: reduction, core, onlay, load, then bar after the peak and a mint pulse hold. The sphere lifts during D and the onlay drops 1.4 mm, so they never collide.
- Focus sequencing: every bar fills after its load peak, with an amber signal wire from the lingual gauge to the chart and an amber outline on the filling bar.
- End of reel: E has a plotted 1-vs-6 pictogram over a 0.65 feathered full-sheet dim, and a larger 6× (184 px). Reduction markup is clipped to the outline. Final box spacing is now kicker y0+50, headline y0+128, sub y0+168 at 32 px with its own fade.
