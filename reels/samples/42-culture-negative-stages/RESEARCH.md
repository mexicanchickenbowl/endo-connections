## 1. The three proportions (the numbers on the donuts)

Quantity: **the proportion of cases (sampled teeth / canals) that returned a NEGATIVE culture** at that stage.
It is not "percent of bacteria killed" and not "percent of bacteria remaining".

| Stage | Chapter 17 (short quote) | 2023 review (short quote) | Resident's brief | Match? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Mechanical preparation, water or saline only | "negative cultures were achieved on a weighted pooled average in 31% of the cases (range 0% to 79%)" | "negative cultures were achieved in 31% (range 0%–79%) (weighted pooled average) of the cases" | 31%, range 0–79% | yes |
| B. Mechanical preparation + NaOCl (0.5–5%), immediately after debridement | "the frequency of negative cultures immediately increased to a weighted pooled average of 52% (range 13% to 95%)" | "increased the frequency of negative cultures immediately after debridement to 52% (range 13%–95%) (weighted pooled average)" | 52%, range 13–95% | yes |
| C. Subsequent visit, after an active interappointment antibacterial dressing | "negative cultures in the subsequent visit were achieved on average in 71% of cases (range 25% to 100%)" | "at the subsequent visit to an average of 71% (range 25%–100%) (weighted pooled average) of cases" | 71%, range 25–100% | yes |

Details recorded from the sources:

- **Kind of number.** A and B: "weighted pooled average" in both sources. C: the chapter says "on average"; the 2023
  review adds "(weighted pooled average)". Neither source describes the weighting method or lists which table rows
  entered each pool, so the pooled values cannot be re-derived exactly from what was read.
- **Unit.** Both sources say "of (the) cases". Table 17.9's column header is "percentage of samples with bacterial
  presence"; its sample-size column is mostly teeth, sometimes canals (e.g. Gomes 1996 "42 root canals", Chu 2006
  "88 canals", Wang 2007 "43 canals", Card 2002 "teeth/canals"). On screen the unit is therefore "cases", with a
  fine-print gloss "case = a sampled tooth or canal".
- **Table 17.9 reports culture-POSITIVE percentages** ("bacterial presence") at baseline, after preparation ± irrigation
  and at the next visit (after dressing ±). The narrative converts to negative cultures. Negative % = 100 − positive %.
- **NaOCl concentration:** "concentration range 0.5% to 5%" (chapter); "0.5%–5.0%" (review).
- **Timing of sampling.** A and B: at the end of the preparation visit ("immediately", "immediately after debridement").
  C: "in the subsequent visit" after an "active interappointment antibacterial dressing".
- **What "dressing" includes (Table 17.9, next-visit column):** calcium hydroxide (most rows), camphorated phenol /
  camphorated (para)monochlorophenol, calcium hydroxide + CPMC, clindamycin, iodine–potassium iodide, 2% chlorhexidine,
  chlorhexidine + calcium hydroxide, antibiotic/steroid pastes (Septomixine, Ledermix). So C is "any active antibacterial
  dressing", not calcium hydroxide alone.
- **Number of studies.** The chapter sentence cites 40 reference numbers and Table 17.9 has 40 study rows (1953–2013).
  The 2023 review lists about the same studies by name plus a systematic review (Siqueira & Rôças 2008). The review
  attributes the three pooled figures to Gulabivala & Ng 2019 (a chapter in Ingle's Endodontics and a Br Dent J paper);
  those two were not read.
- **Culture reversals.** Both sources state that most studies report culture reversals between appointments when no
  active dressing is used (regrowth or coronal leakage). This is why the three values are not a simple staircase.
- **Sterility caveat.** The chapter describes a negative culture as confirming absence of bacteria "in the part of the
  root canal system that could be sampled"; the review says "(sample-able part of the)". A negative culture is a
  below-detection result for the sampled part, not proof of a sterile canal system.

Both figures as the resident stated them: **verified**. These are odds ratios for healing by culture result — a different
quantity from the proportions of cases with a negative culture. They are kept out of the animation and appear only in
the caption and NOTES.

## Do not claim

- Do not say "percent of bacteria killed / removed / remaining". The quantity is the share of cases with a negative culture.
- Do not present 31 → 52 → 71 as one cohort followed through treatment, or say that a step "adds" 21 or 19 points.
  The pools are different sets of studies, methods, decades and sampling times.
- Do not say a negative culture means a sterile canal. It means no growth from the sampled part of the canal.
- Do not say "canals" as the unit without qualification; the sources say "cases" (mostly teeth, sometimes canals).
- Do not say "calcium hydroxide" for stage C; the dressings pooled are mixed.
- Do not say all three are "weighted pooled averages in the chapter"; the chapter words the third as "on average".
- Do not recommend culturing every canal; the chapter notes the practice has fallen out of clinical favor.
- Do not attach either odds ratio to the pies, and do not quote "twofold" without the source and the 2023 re-estimate.
- Do not cite primary studies on screen; only a sample was checked, and only at abstract level.

## Discrepancies with the resident's brief

1. **Unit.** Brief: "canals/samples". Sources: "cases" (text) and "samples" (table header), counted mostly as teeth.
   On screen: "cases", with "case = a sampled tooth or canal" in fine print.
2. **Third value's wording.** In the chapter the 71% is "on average", not explicitly a weighted pooled average; the
   2023 review labels all three as weighted pooled averages. The number and range match.
3. **Table 17.9 shows positives, not negatives.** The three negative-culture figures are in the paragraph after the
   table (print p. 680), not in the table itself.
4. **Likely underlying studies.** Byström & Sundqvist 1981/1983/1985, Sjögren 1991/1997, Dalton 1998, Shuping 2000,
   Card 2002, Peters 2002, Ørstavik 1991 and Siqueira 2007 (two papers) are in Table 17.9. McGurkin-Smith 2005 is not.
5. **Odds ratios.** Both verified as stated. Extra observation: the 2023 text says "twofold" next to a figure that
   reports 1.6.
6. **Small table/abstract mismatches:** Byström & Sundqvist 1981 (table 15 teeth, abstract 17); Wang 2007 (table 3/36,
   abstract 4/36). Neither affects the on-screen numbers.

Nothing in the brief was contradicted: 31 / 52 / 71, the three ranges, the NaOCl concentration range and both odds
ratios are all confirmed in the sources read.
