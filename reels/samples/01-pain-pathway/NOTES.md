**Output:** `reels/out/01-pain-pathway.mp4` (29.6 s, 1080×1920, 30 fps) and `reels/out/01-pain-pathway.jpg` (poster at t = 2.2 s, the hook frame).

## Concept
A silent "signal race". An intense cold stimulus hits a lower first molar and reaches the pulp, so both fiber types fire. Two impulses leave the pulp together: a fast **Aδ** (cyan, myelinated, hopping node to node) and a slow **C** (magenta, unmyelinated, continuous). A label on the C impulse says that C fibers fire only if the pulp itself is reached. The impulses follow the real relay chain: pulp, inferior alveolar n. (V3), trigeminal ganglion (cell bodies, no synapse), spinal trigeminal tract, subnucleus caudalis (synapse 1, the main pain relay), midline crossing, VPM thalamus (synapse 2), cortex. The camera chases the Aδ impulse. Each relay lights up as a signal arrives, and two live HUD cards show speed, a running "to brainstem" clock and race progress. Aδ reaches the S1 face area and the cortex reads **SHARP, well-localized** (a reticle around the molar region). C then catches up (still slow-motion, about ×15), branches to a broad deep/medial region labelled **S2 + cingulate**, and the scene reads **DULL, poorly localized**. Fine print says that in real teeth the dull pain lags by *seconds*, mostly the time the stimulus takes to reach the pulp, not conduction speed. Payoff: convergence of many afferents onto the same caudalis neurons *partly* explains why the ache is hard to pin to one tooth.

### Corrections to the brief
- Conduction velocities are shown as **typical ranges with "~"**, now backed by pulp-specific single-unit data (Jyväsjärvi & Kniffki 1987). Pulpal nerves span a continuum (Cadden 1983), so no single number is presented as the truth.
- The brief's "A-delta = reversible pulpitis, C = irreversible pulpitis" mapping is not used. The Aδ card names the stimulus class it responds to ("dentin stimuli: cold, air, probe") and the C card is hedged ("likely the ache of pulpitis"), with fine print that inflammation also sensitizes A fibers.
- **Cause of the first/second pain gap in teeth:** in the reel the race shows the (real but small, ~0.14 s in this toy model) conduction difference. On screen it is made explicit that C fibers fire only when the pulp itself is reached and that, in real teeth, the dull pain lags by *seconds*, mostly because the stimulus must reach the pulp (Jyväsjärvi 1987; Mengel 1993; Närhi 1992/1994). Närhi 1992 also notes first and second pain are less clearly separated in teeth than in a limb.
- "Especially subnucleus caudalis" is kept, worded as the *main* pain relay; the rostral-subnuclei nuance is documented above.
- "S1 cortex" applies to first pain; second pain is routed to S2 + cingulate (Ploner 2002).

## What is schematic or exaggerated (flagged on screen as "schematic · not to scale" and "toy model … clocks: periphery only")
- Lateral projection: midline brainstem, lateral ganglion and mandible are drawn in one plane, so the route forms a hairpin (up to the ganglion, down to caudalis). Landmark positions are approximate; the lower first molar is drawn about 1.2× size.
- Myelin internodes and the node-to-node "hop" are drastically enlarged and slowed (saltatory conduction as a metaphor).
- Path length (15 cm), representative speeds (15 and 1 m/s) and both slow-motion factors are a toy model. Central (2nd/3rd-order) conduction is not timed.
- The medulla cross-section is a cartoon; "to thalamus" is drawn as an upward arrow although the tract actually ascends out of the plane of the section.
- Only four convergent inputs are shown, as examples.
- The "SHARP" reticle, the "DULL" clouds and the "S2 + cingulate" glow are perceptual/anatomical metaphors, not imaging data.

## Design rationale
- **Style: NEON NEURO.** Deep navy-to-black radial background (with a static 0–4 level luminance dither against banding after Instagram's re-encode), faint parallax dot lattice and drifting dust (drift and twinkle periodic in the loop length). Thin periwinkle line-art head (sagittal projection; base stroke ~2 px at z = 1; the neck fades out softly) with gyri, cerebellar folia, brainstem, mandible and individually drawn teeth/pulps. Additive "light" rendering of the world layer plus a two-scale bloom (¼ and 1/10 resolution blurred copies composited with `lighter`). Cyan (#38EEFF) = Aδ, magenta (#FF42CC) = C, lighter tints for 2nd/3rd-order neurons, lavender = convergent inputs.
- **Type:** Space Grotesk for titles/captions (Inter supplies the Greek δ), JetBrains Mono for HUD, tags, readouts (including the big speed numerals, so "1" is unambiguous) and fine print. Titles 66–118 px, captions 40 px, every must-read label ≥ 30 px (HUD stations, "to brainstem", pills, inset labels); 22–26 px only for fine print, ranges and citations.
- **One focal point at a time:** the camera chases the leading impulse; the Aδ pulse waits at caudalis while the inset plays (main view dimmed); the Aδ visuals dim during the C catch-up; the convergence beat dims the main wires to 20 %. Captions say what *happens* at each relay (the HUD route map names the stations). Labels are anchored to world points with dashed leaders, clamped into the safe stage and shown only while relevant; off-screen racers get an edge arrow.
- **Infographic devices:** top route map (5 relays, dual progress rails), two live fiber cards (speed, "to brainstem" ms clock that locks on arrival, progress track with ganglion tick), and a time-scale pill (SLOW-MO ×440, then SLOW-MO ~×15).
- **Insets:** a medulla cross-section (L/R marks, crossing axon, "to thalamus") and a "many inputs → one neuron" diagram.
- Motion is all eased (camera zoom interpolated in log space, 3 % push-ins on hold beats). No flashing: the DULL clouds breathe at ~0.55 Hz with low amplitude. All text stays inside x 64–1016 / y 250–1570 and out of the IG right-hand column (x > 960, y > 1100).
- Deterministic: no `Math.random`/clock use (`Kit.rng`, `Kit.noise1`, `Kit.hash`). The whole frame is one canvas (fast capture path, ~65 ms/frame), and every font weight used is preloaded via `REEL.fonts`.
