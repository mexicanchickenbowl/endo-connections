# 30 · Resorption 2/5 · External surface + pressure resorption — RISOGRAPH DUOTONE PRINT

## Concept
Two kinds of external resorption that stop when their cause stops. Printed as three two-color riso "sheets"
(fluorescent pink + teal, overprint = dark third tone, halftone tints, grain, slight misregistration) pulled off a stack.
A persistent CAUSE switch -> CLASTS dial shows the same thing for both kinds: cause off, clasts stop (with a lag).

## On-screen claims and sources
All sources were checked by the research and fact-check stages (RESEARCH.md); nothing new was added at build time.
1. Small localized injury -> small, shallow resorption of cementum; self-limiting. Abbott PV, Lin S. Dent Traumatol 2022;38:267-285 (PMID 35605161), full text section 6.1.
2. No infection -> does not persist; repaired by new cementum over "a few weeks" (narrative-review figure; no timed human study, so the clock has no numerals). Abbott & Lin 2022; Lin S et al. Dent Traumatol 2022;38:253-266 (PMID 35559593), full text.
3. Pressure is the stimulus that keeps resorption going (orthodontic treatment, impacted tooth, tumor). Fuss Z, Tsesis I, Lin S. Dent Traumatol 2003;19:175-82 (PMID 12848710), abstract.
4. Pressure removed -> resorption stops, cemental repair follows; resorption is irreversible (root length does not return). Patel S, Saberi N, Pimental T, Teng PH. Int Endod J 2022;55 Suppl 4:892-921 (PMID 35229320), full text (relevant sections) + abstract.
5. Pulp tests normal; managed by eliminating the cause. Patel et al. 2022, Table 1 and text.
6. Surface resorption: usually not evident on a radiograph. Abbott & Lin 2022, section 6.1.
7. Orthodontic: shortened, blunted, rounded apex. Abbott & Lin 2022 section 6.6; Patel et al. 2022.

## Schematic / not literal
- Cells, their size, number and timing; vessel-to-surface recruitment; the starburst "injury".
- Clast size on the whole-tooth sheet and the amount of root lost (about 19% of length) are exaggerated for visibility.
- The stop is drawn as a wind-down, not instant; in reality some lesions progress (7 of 32 in Falahat 2008) - caption only.
- The intrusion arrow is one example of orthodontic force. Radiographs are icons; pink pulp = vital, not radiographic density.
- "schematic · not to scale" is on every sheet inside the safe area.

## Deliberately left off screen (see RESEARCH.md "Do not claim")
No healing time in numbers, no stats (Ericson & Kurol 38%, Lund 6.6% go in the caption or nowhere), no "never visible",
no "pulp never involved", no apex regrowth, no bacteria.

## Design rationale
Pink = activity (clasts, force, cause ON, living pulp); teal = tissue and type; overprint dark = new cementum, nuclei, emphasis.
Small text is teal only so misregistration never blurs it. Files: index.html, riso.js (print pipeline), reel.js (content).
