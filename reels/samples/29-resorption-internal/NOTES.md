# 29 — Internal inflammatory root resorption · layered papercraft / shadowbox

Resorption series 1/5. Silent, 22.4 s, 1080x1920, one 2D canvas (`index.html` + `reel.js`), deterministic `render(t)`.
Evidence brief: `RESEARCH.md` (researched + independently fact-checked). Only its section (b) "recommended on-screen
facts" is on screen, with its wording constraints and its "Do not claim" list. No number is shown.

## Concept
A shadowbox: cream mat, dusty-pink and teal inner mats, and behind them cut-paper layers at different depths
(bone with punched marrow spaces, a gingiva band, the tooth) that slide against each other as the camera pans and zooms.
The tooth is a paper section: enamel sheet, dentin sheet with the canal cut out of it, a dusty-mauve predentin liner
and a coral pulp inlay. The story is told by handling the paper: a flap of dentin lifts along a dashed cut line (the
loss to come); the pulp layer is lifted (hinged at the apex, shadow on the dentin) and its coronal part turns gray
and speckled (necrotic, infected) while the apical part stays coral with red thread "vessels"; the liner peels off
mid-root; teal paper odontoclasts gather on the bare wall and nibble the canal into a symmetric oval; the pulp layer
is pulled out by a paper tab, the threads snap back, the cells curl and drop, and the hole stays exactly as large as
it was. Labels are paper tabs with a color stripe that matches the piece they name. The radiograph is a film card
laid over the paper tooth in register (the same cut-outs in gray paper); on the cone shift the film image slides and
a dashed ghost of the old root outline stays behind.

Wording guards taken from the fact-check: coronal pulp is "necrotic, infected" (not "inflamed"); "blood supply" (the
cells' route is only animated); "well-defined" not "smooth"; "classic" heads the sign list; no prevalence, success
rate, growth rate or pulp-test statement; no pink spot; no on-screen source line names an unread classic (Gartner
1976 is in meta.json claim 8 only, marked "not read"); Patel 2010 is not printed under the mechanism, parallax or
treatment scenes; "stops", never "heals"; the defect is never shown shrinking; MTA is given as an example ("e.g.");
nothing enters from the periodontal ligament; on the film nothing can be traced through the lesion (that is the
external-resorption sign, Thomas 2014).

Caption-only nuance (meta.json): "Internal (inflammatory)"; "Classic model:" in front of the two requirements (the
same Patel 2022 review shows a case after a Biodentine pulpotomy with no necrotic coronal pulp, and a self-limiting
transient phase); "Root canal treatment removes both"; the rare replacement type looks irregular and mottled (Patel
2022; "very rare", Abbott & Lin 2022); 2-D signs are fallible; evidence level.

## What is schematic (fine-print label `schematic` is on screen throughout)
- Mid-root position of the lesion (a drawing choice: Patel 2010 "middle and apical thirds", Patel 2022 "anywhere").
- The perfect symmetry and the clean oval: real defects "can be any shape or size" (Abbott & Lin 2022).
- The tidy straight boundary between necrotic and vital pulp, the speed of the necrotic front, microbe confetti.
- Route of infection not shown: the crown is drawn intact (caries, cracks, fractures, trauma are the usual routes;
  Patel 2022, Abbott & Lin 2022).
- The apical pulp is vital but inflamed (Abbott & Lin 2022: "inflamed pulp tissue in the root canal apical to the
  resorptive defect"); it is drawn in the same coral as the healthy pulp of the hook. "Vital" is the claim, not "healthy".
- The hook flap (dentin lifting along the cut line) is a paper preview of the loss, not a biological event.
- Number (six), size and nuclei of the clastic cells; small cells arriving from the apical vessels and settling
  (Nilsson 2013 states this origin outright; Patel 2022 notes a possible periodontal origin — unsettled).
- Rate of growth; the scalloped "bites" (they relax into a well-defined outline when growth stops).
- Predentin thickness, canal width and all proportions (canal exaggerated); thread vessels and their branching.
- Treatment beat: the pulp leaving in one piece; the cells staying on the wall and dropping afterwards (in reality
  the granulation tissue with its cells is removed chemo-mechanically: Nilsson 2013; hypochlorite activation and
  calcium hydroxide, Patel 2022); no instrumentation/irrigation shown; the main tooth left unfilled (obturation is
  only shown in the perforation inset).
- Perforation inset: site and shape of the perforation; only the defect and the apical canal are in view.
- Film card: an idealized 2-D image in gray paper; the amount and direction of image shift; the bone layer sliding
  further than the tooth (parallax flavor); the ghost outline. There is no contrast case of an external lesion moving
  off the canal. 2-D films cannot reliably separate internal from external cervical resorption (caption).

## Design rationale
- Style commitment: every element is a paper piece with a soft drop shadow and a cut-edge bevel (light from top
  left), filled with a grain + fiber texture; outlines are resampled into straight "snips" of irregular length for
  a scissor-cut look; labels are tabs that slide out from under the mat; fasteners are brass brads; leaders are
  paper strips.
- Palette: cream, coral, dusty pink, dusty mauve, teal, charcoal, with mustard only for microbes/brads.
  Color carries meaning consistently: coral = vital tissue, charcoal gray = necrotic, teal = clastic cells,
  mauve = predentin, speckled white with a mauve cut edge = calcium silicate cement, hatched salmon = gutta-percha.
- Parallax: each layer has a depth; pan, zoom and a slow sway move deeper layers less. The cone-shift demo uses the
  same trick inside the film — the oval is cut into the same sheet as the canal and cannot leave it.
- Type: Fraunces (title, header, role lines in italic, numerals) + Nunito (labels). American spelling.
- Layout: tooth on the right (clear of the button column), tabs in the left column inside x 64-505; film mount ends
  at x 958; nothing must-read below y 1580 or above y 230; tag and handle sit 16 px below the top of the 3:4 crop.
