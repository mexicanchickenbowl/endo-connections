# 40 · Eight conditions associated with better tooth survival after root canal treatment

**Style:** celestial star chart. One Canvas 2D frame: an antique astronomical plate on a deep indigo sky, gold-foil line
work, a double-ruled border with a graduated scale, faint coordinate circles, hour lines and a dashed ecliptic, a compass
rose, a slowly turning armillary sphere, tiny background stars on three parallax depths. Playfair Display 700/600
(title), Libre Baskerville 400/700 (condition names, lining numerals), EB Garamond italic 500/600 and 400 (subtitle,
handle, source line).
**Duration:** 30 s · 1080×1920 · silent · poster at 28.0 s (the complete catalog of eight). American spelling.
**Files:** `index.html` (content, timeline, sky, plate, constellation, caption, catalog, `render(t)`), `pictos.js`
(the eight engraved pictograms). Evidence brief: `RESEARCH.md`.

**What this is:** a board/quiz study animation that restates one summary list from a textbook chapter (Ng & Gulabivala,
"Evaluation of Outcomes", ch. 17 in Cohen's Pathways of the Pulp, 12th ed.). It is about TOOTH SURVIVAL (the tooth stays
in the mouth), not periapical healing; healing rates and periapical lesions are not mentioned anywhere on screen.
Sibling reels 39 (surgery factors) and 41 (periapical-healing factors) cover the other lists.

## Concept
Eight stars are plotted one at a time on a chart and joined by gold lines into one constellation whose outline is a
two-rooted tooth (cusp, central fossa, cusp, cervical line, apex, furcation, apex, cervical line). A faint engraved tooth
figure sits behind the stars, the way atlas plates draw a figure behind a constellation, and grows clearer as stars are
added. Each star is one condition: a numeral beside the star, the condition name in the caption band under the plate,
and an engraved pictogram in a round medallion (bottom left of the plate). A dial at top right counts "n / 8" and lights
one of eight pips per star. When the eighth star is in, the outline closes, the plate folds up and the catalog of all
eight appears under it, with the eight pictograms as small numbered medallions beside the constellation.

Star positions are a drawing device only. No star position means anything anatomically about its condition (for example,
star 1 "nonmolar tooth" sits on a cusp of a molar-shaped figure). The numbering 1 to 8 follows the chapter's order.

Framing, on screen for the whole reel (title block): "EIGHT CONDITIONS associated with better TOOTH SURVIVAL after root
canal treatment · associations, not guarantees". No causal verbs anywhere; no star-lore or destiny wording.

## On-screen statements, chapter wording and evidence basis
Chapter wording is quoted from the bullet list under "Summary of Factors Influencing the Survival of Teeth Following Root
Canal Treatment", which opens: "The following conditions have been found to significantly improve tooth survival
following root canal treatment". Reference numbers are the chapter's own.

| # | On screen | Chapter bullet | Evidence the chapter cites | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Nonmolar tooth | "Nonmolar teeth (Fig. 17.27)" | Fig. 17.27 forest plot, nonmolar vs molar, pooled OR 1.4 (95% CI 1.1, 1.6); ref 149 (Ng, Mann & Gulabivala, Int Endod J 2010;43:171-89, PMID 20158529) | matches chapter |
| 2 | Mesial and distal adjacent teeth present | "Teeth with both mesial and distal adjacent teeth (Fig. 17.28)" | Fig. 17.28, both contacts present vs absent, pooled OR 2.6 (95% CI 1.8, 3.7); refs 149, 151 | matches chapter ("both" is carried by "mesial and distal ... present") |
| 3 | Not distal-most in the arch | "Teeth not located as the distal-most tooth in the arch" | ref 151 (Ng, Mann & Gulabivala, Int Endod J 2011;44:610-25, PMID 21366627): "terminal location of the tooth" | matches chapter |
| 4 | Molar with a cast restoration | "Teeth (molar) with cast restorations after treatment (Fig. 17.29)" | Fig. 17.29, cast vs plastic restoration, pooled OR 3.5 (95% CI 2.6, 4.7); refs 149, 151, 189 | matches chapter, "(molar)" qualifier kept |
| 5 | No cast post and core needed | "Teeth not requiring cast post and core for support and retention of restoration" | refs 111 (Lazarski 2001), 151 | matches chapter |
| 6 | Not a fixed-prosthesis abutment | "Teeth not functioning as abutments for fixed prosthesis" | refs 3 (Alley 2004), 111 (Lazarski 2001), 191 (Salvi 2007), 151 | matches chapter; see caveat below |

No numbers (odds ratios, survival percentages) are shown on screen.

### Caveats that are in the chapter but not on screen (carried by the caption / this file)
- Item 6: the chapter's body text says of Ng 2011 that abutment teeth "had poorer survival rates" but that n = 94 was too
  small "to be considered statistically significant". The item rests on the other cited studies and on the 2010
  meta-analysis. In the Ng 2011 abstract, abutment status is not among the thirteen significant factors.
- Item 1: tooth type is not among the thirteen factors in the Ng 2011 abstract either; in the chapter it comes from the
  pooled analysis (Fig. 17.27, Ng 2010).
- Items 2 and 3: the chapter notes that proximal contacts and terminal position "were significantly correlated with
  molar teeth".
- Item 4: the body text and Fig. 17.29 speak of crowns or cast restorations in general; the authors call crowning every
  root-treated tooth "probably a gross exaggeration of the true need". Ng 2011 compared cast with temporary restoration.
- Item 7: body text specifies probing defects "of endodontic origin"; Ng 2011 says "narrow but deep" probing depth and
  lists perforation as a preoperative factor for retreatment cases only. Preoperative cervical resorption is named in
  the body text but is not one of the eight bullets.
- Item 8: in Ng 2011 both effects are time-dependent (patency: lower tooth loss in the first 22 months only; extrusion
  of gutta-percha: higher hazard only after 22 months).
- Also in the section but outside the eight bullets: diabetes and systemic steroid therapy (higher chance of
  extraction).

## What is schematic
- The constellation and the engraved tooth figure are decoration; star positions carry no anatomical meaning.
- Pictograms are simplified icons, not to scale. Pictogram 7 shows only the probing defect although the item lists four
  preoperative conditions. Pictogram 3 highlights an arbitrary non-terminal tooth. A red stroke through a pictogram
  means "absent / not this", matching the "No ..." or "Not ..." wording of items 3, 5, 6 and 7.
- The armillary sphere, compass rose, coordinate grid and background stars are ornament.

## What could not be verified
- The book title and edition number are not printed in the PDF text (only "17 Evaluation of Outcomes", the authors,
  "Part III" and page 652 are). Chapter 17 / p. 652 is consistent with the 12th edition; "12th ed." comes from the brief.
- Ng 2011 and Ng 2010 were read as PubMed abstracts only, not full text.
- Chapter references 3, 111, 189, 191 were identified from the chapter's reference list; their abstracts were not read.
